Kando is the phone and tablet companion of Kando for macOS, Windows and Linux, which is free, with its source code public (https://github.com/yldm-tech/Kando). The app has no account of its own and talks only to the computers it is paired with: it shows their libraries and copies their films over the local network. Word lookups and line explanations started on the device are sent to the paired computer, and the desktop Kando app there sends them to the AI service the user chose in its Settings. That service may be EveryAPI, an AI service run by YLDM Co., Ltd., the developer of Kando, or another provider or a local model the user set up. The phone app itself sells nothing, shows no prices and links to no purchase.

To try it with a computer:
1. Install Kando on a Mac, a Windows or a Linux computer on the same Wi-Fi network as the device, and add a video file of your own to its library; Kando recognizes its speech, and translates it once an AI service is set up in its Settings.
2. On the computer, open Kando's Settings › Phone Sync and turn it on. It shows an address and a code.
3. On the device, go through the introduction to its last step, Connect your computer (or, after skipping it, tap Connect a Computer in the Library tab or in Settings), enter the address and the code, tap Pair, and allow access to the local network when iOS asks.

The computer's films then show in the Library tab: play one, replay a line, save words and lines, and review them in the Learn tab. Without a paired computer the app shows how to connect one; Skip at the top of the introduction, or Skip for Now on its last step, opens it without pairing.

The app asks for local network access to reach the paired computer, for the camera only when the user taps the button that reads the QR code the computer shows on its Phone Sync page, and for notifications only when the user turns on the daily review reminder in Settings. It collects no data.
