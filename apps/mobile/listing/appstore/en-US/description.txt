Kando helps you learn a language from films in that language. Kando on your computer recognizes the speech in your videos and translates it line by line; this app brings those films to your phone and tablet, with the original line and its translation on screen together.

WATCH
• Bilingual subtitles: both lines, the original only, the translation only, or a hidden line you tap to reveal.
• Replay the line you didn't catch, loop it, or step to the previous or next line.
• Shadowing holds after each line so you can say it before the film plays on.
• Change the speed, set a sleep timer, listen with the picture off, and move the subtitles earlier or later.
• Pick up where you left off, on your phone or on your computer.

LEARN
• Tap a word to look it up, or have a line explained, through the AI service you set up in Kando on your computer.
• Save words and lines while you watch, and write notes on them.
• Review what you saved when it's due. A daily goal, your streak and an optional evening reminder keep you going.

TAKE THEM WITH YOU
• Keep films on your phone and watch them without a connection. They copy from your computer over your local network.
• See what your computer is working on, and when a film is ready.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account and no tracking. The app talks only to the computers you pair it with, over your local network, encrypted and pinned to the computer you paired.

Kando needs Kando on a computer running macOS, Windows or Linux, which is free and open source: github.com/yldm-tech/Kando

Kando speaks English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese and Korean.
